Choose a recipient to start a call.

If a call drops temporarily while both participants remain connected to Waltajjii, either person can request an ICE restart. Reconnection is not guaranteed on every network.

Call quality monitoring starts when a call begins. Metrics are measured on this device only and are not stored.

Live call captions

Captions are generated by your browser's speech-recognition feature from your microphone. Depending on your browser, audio may be processed by its speech service. Waltajjii does not record or save this audio or caption history. Sharing is optional and sends caption text only to the person in this active call.

Translation is a separate action: the latest caption text is sent to the configured translation service. The translation result is shown only on your device and is not shared with the caller.

When enabled, each eligible incoming caption is sent as text to the configured translation service. Translation is shown only on your device. Automatic translation is limited to 30 captions per call and at most one request every 1.5 seconds; skipped captions are not queued.

Start a voice or video call to enable captions.

Captions will appear here when enabled.
